Policies

Shipping

We currently ship across India only. Orders are packed and dispatched within 24-48 hours of confirmation, and delivery typically takes 4-7 business days depending on your pincode.

  • Free shipping on orders above the minimum order value shown at checkout
  • A flat shipping fee applies below that value
  • You can track progress under Account → Order History — every order shows a live status timeline (Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered)

Returns & Exchange

We want you to love your saree, not just accept it. If something's off, you have 7 days from delivery to request a return or exchange, provided:

  • The saree is unused, unwashed, and unaltered
  • Original tags and packaging are intact
  • The blouse piece has not been cut

To start one: go to Account → Order History and press Order Inquiry on the delivered order — or use the Order inquiry button in your delivery e-mail (no login needed, handy if you checked out as a guest). Choose the saree(s), tell us what is wrong and add 3 photos taken with your camera. Our team reviews it and replies by e-mail. If it is not approved, you get one more chance to send a clearer inquiry; after that the decision is final. If it is approved, we tell you how to send the saree back and refund you to your original payment method once it reaches us.

Cancellations

Orders can be self-cancelled from Account → Order History for free as long as they're still in the "Confirmed" or "Packed" stage. Once an order ships, it can no longer be cancelled — please use the return process instead after delivery.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of us receiving the returned item.

Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you create a Padmora account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address. When you place an order, we store your order details and the address it was shipped to.

How We Use It

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To let you track order status and view order history
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via Contact Us
  • To improve our catalog and customer experience

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your password is stored as a one-way cryptographic hash and is never visible to us in plain text.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in between visits (a session token) and to remember items in your bag. We don't use third-party tracking cookies.

Your Rights

You can update your profile and saved address anytime from your Account page. To request full account deletion, contact us via the Contact Us page.
