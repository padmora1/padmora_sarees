Saree Sizing & Fit Guide

General guidance that applies across our sarees — check the individual product for its exact length.

Saree length

Most of our sarees are woven to the standard 5.5–6.5m length (this one: 6.3m of body + a separate blouse piece). That drapes comfortably for most heights up to about 5'8"; if you're petite, your tailor can pleat the pallu shorter without altering the fabric.

Blouse piece

The blouse piece ships as unstitched fabric (typically 0.8m) — it needs a local tailor to stitch it to your blouse size. 0.8m comfortably covers most sizes up to a 40" bust; if you're above that, ask your tailor whether a panel insert is needed before cutting.

Petticoat

Not included with any of our sarees. For the cleanest drape, choose a petticoat close to the saree's base colour, and have it fitted to sit just above your ankles.

Care

Dry clean only, unless the product page says otherwise — handloom and block-printed fabrics can bleed or lose their finish in a home wash.
