Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you create a Padmora account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address. When you place an order, we store your order details and the address it was shipped to.

How We Use It

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To let you track order status and view order history
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via Contact Us
  • To improve our catalog and customer experience

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your password is stored as a one-way cryptographic hash and is never visible to us in plain text.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in between visits (a session token) and to remember items in your bag. We don't use third-party tracking cookies.

Your Rights

You can update your profile and saved address anytime from your Account page. To request full account deletion, contact us via the Contact Us page.
