How We Began

Started with one Maheshwari saree, not a boardroom

Padmora began with Yashi and team's own wardrobe frustration: it was nearly impossible to buy a genuine handloom saree online without wondering if it was really handloom at all. Power-loom imitations sold at handloom prices are common, and most buyers have no way to tell the difference until the saree arrives.

So Yashi and team started walking into the weaving and printing clusters directly — Maheshwar, Ajrakhpur, Paithan, Narayanpet — and building relationships with the families who actually sit at the loom or the printing table. Every saree we list carries the name of the weaver or artisan house behind it, and we say plainly when a technique is handloom, handcrafted, or powerloom. No fabric here is sold as something it isn't.
